For the provision of digital services «XDEVS»

1. General provisions

1.1. This document is a public offer by the developer operating under the brand «XDEVS» (hereinafter — the Contractor) regarding software development, scripts, and digital content services.

1.2. Acceptance of this offer is confirmed by the Client making a prepayment or submitting an order form via the official website.

2. Subject of the agreement

2.1. The Contractor undertakes to provide IT development services according to the agreed Technical Specification (TS) or order details, and the Client undertakes to accept and pay for these services.

3. Rights and obligations of the parties

3.1. The Contractor commits to delivering high-quality work within the agreed timeframe.

3.2. The Client commits to providing all necessary materials, access, and clear requirements in a timely manner.

4. Service fees and payment procedure

4.1. Service fees are determined according to the current pricelist: xeraze.github.io/Pricelist.

4.2. Special terms. Work performed by the Contractor within the non-commercial project «Sloboda Hospital [RP]» is unpaid (free of charge) at the Contractor's personal initiative. This condition does not apply to any orders from third parties.

4.3. Work on paid orders begins only after the Client pays a prepayment of 25% of the total cost.

4.4. The prepayment guarantees the booking of the developer's time.

5. Procedure for delivery and acceptance

5.1. Demonstration of intermediate and final results is provided via screenshots, video recording, or the Contractor's test environment.

5.2. The Client has the right to request free revisions only if they do not contradict the originally approved Technical Specification. Any new features requested after development begins are billed separately.

6. Intellectual property and file transfer

6.1. Pursuant to Articles 433 and 435 of the Civil Code of Ukraine, computer programs and source code are objects of copyright. Copyright belongs to the Contractor from the moment the work is created.

6.2. The Contractor retains all economic and non-economic copyrights to the source code until the Client has paid 100% of the service fees.

6.3. Delivery of source files (.lua, .rbxl, .json, etc.) to the Client occurs only after full payment of the remaining balance (75%).

7. Liability and rights protection

7.1. The Client may not present the received code as their own (plagiarism) or resell it to third parties without the Contractor's explicit written consent.

7.2. If the product is used without full payment, the Contractor reserves the right to send a DMCA Notice to platform administrators (Roblox, Discord, GitHub) to have the content blocked.

7.3. The Contractor is not liable for product malfunctions caused by the Client's unauthorized modifications to the source code after delivery, or by failures of third-party platforms.

8. Force Majeure

8.1. The Parties are released from liability for partial or complete failure to fulfill their obligations due to circumstances of insuperable force (natural disasters, global internet outages, or platform blockages making work impossible).

9. Modification and termination of the agreement

9.1. The agreement may be terminated by mutual consent of the Parties.

9.2. If the Client unilaterally cancels the project after development has begun, the 25% prepayment is strictly non-refundable, as it covers the time and resources already expended by the Contractor.

9.3. If the Client fails to respond to the Contractor's messages for more than 14 calendar days without prior notice, the Contractor has the right to unilaterally freeze the project or terminate the agreement without refunding the prepayment.

9.4. If the Contractor is unable to complete the project due to their own fault or technical limitations not specified in advance, the Contractor is obliged to refund 100% of the received funds to the Client.

10. Dispute resolution

10.1. All disputes and disagreements arising from this agreement shall be resolved through negotiations via the official XDEVS communication channels (Discord or ticket systems).

11. Final provisions

11.1. The Contractor reserves the right to modify this public offer at any time without prior notice. The new version takes effect immediately upon publication on the website.
